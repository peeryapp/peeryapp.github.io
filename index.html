How Peery works

Nothing to sign up for.

There is no email or password. One recovery phrase, held by you and nobody else, brings everything back.

Files stay on your devices.

Files move between your devices and the people you choose. There is no server of ours holding a copy.

Devices connect directly.*

When a firewall gets in the way, a relay only passes along encrypted bytes it can’t read.

* No middleman for your files: your devices sync directly with each other. To find each other, and to relay traffic when a firewall blocks a direct connection, they can use public Syncthing discovery and relay servers, which can see that devices are connecting but never what’s inside your files.

Simple enough for anyone.

No terminals, no config files, no key ceremonies. Big buttons, plain English and one recovery phrase. Your gran can use it as comfortably as you.
